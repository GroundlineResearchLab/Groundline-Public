## 1. Scope

This license applies to original public research, educational material, documentation, explanatory material, indexes, structured records, and other original outputs published within `Groundline-Public`, unless a specific file states otherwise.

`Groundline-Public` is the public publication layer of Groundline Research Lab.

Groundline Research Lab operates under the DGCP™ Framework.

## 2. Original Material

Original Groundline Research Lab material remains attributable to its recorded author, project, or originating research unit.

Reuse should preserve sufficient attribution to identify the original work.

Where available, attribution should retain:

1. The original title.
2. The author or originating project.
3. Groundline Research Lab.
4. The relevant record identifier.
5. The publication date.
6. The DGCP™ Framework reference where present.
7. A reference to the original public record.

Attribution must not imply endorsement, partnership, authorization, or institutional affiliation that has not been explicitly documented.

## 3. Public Reuse

Public material may be quoted, referenced, studied, discussed, or transformed where permitted by applicable law and the governing policy.

Reuse must not materially misrepresent:

• The original evidence.
• The scope of the research.
• The status of a claim.
• The level of certainty.
• The source context.
• The original conclusion.

Extracts should preserve enough context to avoid changing the meaning of the original record.

## 4. Derivative Material

Derivative or modified material must be clearly distinguishable from the original Groundline Research Lab publication.

A derivative work must not be presented as:

• An unchanged Groundline record.
• An official correction.
• A revised Groundline publication.
• A DGCP™ record.
• An authorized Groundline Research Lab position.

unless such status has been explicitly granted.

Changes made outside the repository do not alter the canonical Groundline record.

## 5. Evidence and Provenance Integrity

Evidence references and provenance information must not be deliberately altered in a way that falsely changes the apparent origin, status, reliability, or meaning of a record.

Where reused material depends on external evidence, the original evidence limitations should remain visible where materially relevant.

Provenance should not be removed when its removal would make verification substantially more difficult or create a misleading representation of the research.

## 6. Third Party Material

External documents, datasets, quotations, images, publications, trademarks, software, and other third party material remain subject to the rights and licenses of their respective owners.

Groundline Research Lab does not grant rights that it does not own.

The presence of third party material within a citation, source list, reference, or research record does not transfer ownership or create a new license for that material.

Users are responsible for determining whether separate permission is required for reuse.

## 7. Data and Datasets

Datasets published within this repository may have additional terms, source restrictions, or attribution requirements.

Where a dataset includes data from external sources, those source conditions continue to apply.

A dataset should not be assumed to be unrestricted merely because it is publicly accessible.

Dataset specific notices take precedence over this general repository statement where explicitly stated.

## 8. Research Status

Publication does not guarantee that a research record is final, complete, or permanently current.

Groundline Research Lab may publish:

• Corrections.
• Updated evidence.
• Revised interpretations.
• Superseding research.
• Methodological changes.

Historical records may remain available for traceability.

A later record does not silently change the historical state of an earlier record.

No guarantee is made that any material is:

Users remain responsible for independent verification appropriate to their use case.

## 10. Repository Integrity

Canonical repository records should preserve their historical and evidentiary context.

Users must not represent externally modified copies as canonical repository records.

Official corrections and revisions are valid only when issued through an authorized Groundline Research Lab publication or repository process.
