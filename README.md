# Groundline-Public

**Groundline Research Lab**

**Public Research and Educational Layer**

**Under the DGCP™ Framework**

## Purpose

`Groundline-Public` is the public publication repository of Groundline Research Lab.

It contains research and educational outputs intended to improve public understanding of data, evidence, provenance, data quality, research literacy, and the systems through which information is created, transformed, verified, and used.

This repository is a publication layer.

It is not the internal research workspace of Groundline Research Lab.

## Scope

Groundline-Public may include work related to:

• Data quality

## Repository Function

The repository exists to translate research into material that can be publicly examined, understood, cited, and reused within the applicable license and source restrictions.

Public outputs should preserve a clear distinction among:

1. Evidence.
2. Observation.
3. Research structure.
4. Interpretation.
5. Conclusion.

Public communication should simplify presentation without removing material qualifications or overstating the evidence.

## Publication Boundary

Material should enter `Groundline-Public` only after it has been classified as suitable for public release.

Internal working notes, incomplete research, private source collections, unresolved hypotheses, internal synthesis, unpublished research logic, and restricted material should remain outside this repository.

Publication does not convert an uncertain claim into a verified claim.

The evidentiary status of the underlying material must remain visible.

## Evidence Standard

Groundline-Public follows an evidence-first approach.

Where relevant, public records should preserve:

• Source identity.
• Publication or observation date.
• Retrieval date where necessary.
• Provenance.
• Relevant document identifiers.
• Research scope.
• Material limitations.
• Distinction between primary and secondary sources.
• Distinction between evidence and interpretation.

A source should not be represented as stronger, more complete, or more authoritative than the source itself supports.

## Provenance

Public research should retain sufficient provenance for readers to understand where information originated and how it entered the research process.

Where practical, records should preserve connections to the underlying research or evidence chain without exposing internal or restricted material.

Provenance supports verification.

It does not by itself establish factual correctness.

## Research Integrity

Groundline-Public follows these principles:

**Evidence before interpretation.**

**Provenance before reuse.**

**Observation before conclusion.**

**Traceability before simplification.**

**Qualification before certainty.**

Corrections should be made transparently.

Historical public records should not be silently rewritten when a later record materially changes the understanding of the subject.

## Public Education

Not every publication in this repository is intended to produce a novel research finding.

Some outputs exist to explain:

• How data is created.
• How evidence is evaluated.
• How sources differ.
• How data changes through processing.
• How uncertainty should be represented.
• How provenance affects trust.
• How research conclusions are constructed.
• How information systems influence public understanding.

Educational outputs must follow the same evidence and provenance discipline as research outputs.

## Relationship to Groundline Research Lab

Groundline Research Lab is the research environment.

`Groundline-Public` is its public publication layer.

Research development, source examination, internal notes, methodological work, and unfinished analysis may occur outside this repository.

Only material approved for public release should be placed here.

## Relationship to DGCP™

Groundline Research Lab operates under the DGCP™ Framework.

DGCP™ provides the broader principles of governance, continuity, proof, traceability, and system structure.

Groundline applies those principles to research, data understanding, evidence, provenance, and public research literacy.

`Groundline-Public` carries those principles into the public publication layer.
